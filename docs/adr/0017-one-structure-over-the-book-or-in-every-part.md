# One Structure per Book, over the Book or anew in every Part

An episodic story wants a Structure that repeats in every Episode, for example a TV-style act structure, and often a season arc over all of them. A Book still follows one Structure, but it now records where that Structure runs: over the whole Book, or anew in every Part, with each Beat's position read as a share of the Part. The season arc is carried by the Book's Outline and its Plot Threads, not by a second Structure. Decided in [More Structures for short and episodic stories, and story archetypes](https://github.com/magnusblombergsson/scaffold/issues/347).

We chose this because "Episode" is already one of the Part labels, so an episodic story is one Manuscript whose Parts are Episodes, and episodic TV mostly carries its season through threads rather than a second set of Beats. One Structure per Book keeps every Beat unambiguous about which pattern it belongs to, and keeps the Plan check to one layer.

## Considered Options

- **Two Structures, one per Episode and one for the season**: matches the shape of a season most closely, but every Beat must say which Structure it belongs to, and the Plan check reads two overlapping layers.
- **Each Part chooses its own Structure**: the most flexible, but every Part gains a detail that no candidate Structure needs, and the check must compare Parts that follow different patterns.
- **Each Episode is a Book in a Series**: needs no change, since each Book has its own Structure and the Series Outline holds the season, but an episodic novel or web serial kept in one volume would be split into many Manuscripts.

## Consequences

- A Book without Parts treats "in every Part" as the whole Book, and the choice is shown only once the Book has a Part.
- No Part can opt out. A prologue Part or a two-part Episode is explained in that Part's Notes, which the Plan check reads as the Author's intent.
- A Structure's fit gains "per Part" beside "per Book" and "across a Series". Like the rest of its fit, this is advice for Plan's shortlist, never a limit.
