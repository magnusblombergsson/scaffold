# Plan may append to Notes; no other Mode may

Notes are the Author's working notes on a Book, Part, Chapter or Scene, and until now no Proposal touched them. Plan may now propose appending to them, never replacing their text; Brainstorm, Interview and Writing still never touch Notes, and no Mode touches private notes or Prose. Decided in [Proposals beyond the Story Bible and Outlines](https://github.com/magnusblombergsson/scaffold/issues/295), charted on [Map: Outline planner](https://github.com/magnusblombergsson/scaffold/issues/292).

We chose this because planning produces material that is not an Outline (why a beat sits where it does, what a structure demands of a Chapter, a reminder for later) and Notes are where the Author already keeps that. Appending only, and only in Plan, keeps the Author's own words unchanged and keeps the other Modes' promise intact.

## Considered Options

- **A separate planning field on each unit**: no Mode would ever write into the Author's Notes, but it adds a second place for working notes that the Author must look in and the Assistant must be sent.
- **Notes Proposals in every Mode**: simpler to state, but Brainstorm and Writing have no reason to write there, and Notes would fill with Assistant text the Author did not ask for.

## Consequences

- An append Proposal to Notes never goes stale; it lands on whatever the Notes hold when accepted and can be undone while they still end with it.
- Which Proposal kinds a Mode may make becomes a per-Mode rule rather than one shared list.
